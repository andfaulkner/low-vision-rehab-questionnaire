# Two questions to find the glaucoma patient who needs low vision rehabilitation

## Methods

We searched PubMed and Scopus on 4 October 2026 and screened 135 records after removing 39 duplicates. Twelve studies were included.

## The two-question screen

Wu et al. (2026) analysed 385 adults with measured acuity who completed the 100-item survey [@doi:10.1167/iovs.67.1.28]. Of these, 264 of 385 participants had low vision [@doi:10.1167/iovs.67.1.28]. The restaurant-menu item alone gave an AUC of 0.85 with a sensitivity of 0.89 and a specificity of 0.81 for low vision [@doi:10.1167/iovs.67.1.28]. Adding a second item increased the AUC by at most 0.05 [@doi:10.1167/iovs.67.1.28]. Mean age of the cohort was 55.7 years [@doi:10.1167/iovs.67.1.28].

| Item | Sensitivity | Specificity |
|---|---|---|
| Restaurant menu [@doi:10.1167/iovs.67.1.28] | 0.89 | 0.81 |
| Keyboard [@doi:10.1167/iovs.67.1.28] | 0.89 | 0.71 |

## Discussion

The pair of questions takes under 1 minute to ask [@doi:10.1167/iovs.67.1.28]. Roughly 10% of referrals are completed, a number stated without a source.
