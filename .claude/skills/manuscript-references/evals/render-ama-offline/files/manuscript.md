# Two questions to find the glaucoma patient who needs low vision rehabilitation

## Introduction

Glaucoma is a leading cause of irreversible low vision, yet few eligible patients reach rehabilitation services, and the American Academy of Ophthalmology recommends that every patient with reduced function be offered referral [@aao-vision-rehab-ppp]. In Canada, legal blindness is defined as acuity of 20/200 or worse or a field of 20 degrees or less [@cnib-legal-blindness]. Self-reported mobility performance correlates with both acuity and field in low vision patients [@doi:10.1111/j.1444-0938.2007.00120.x].

## Methods

We searched PubMed and Scopus on 4 October 2026 and screened 135 records after removing 39 duplicates. Twelve studies were included. A large language model (Claude, Anthropic) produced advisory screening suggestions and first-pass extraction; every decision was made by the reviewer.

## The two-question screen

Wu et al. (2026) analysed 385 adults with measured acuity who completed the 100-item survey [@doi:10.1167/iovs.67.1.28]. Of these, 264 of 385 participants had low vision [@doi:10.1167/iovs.67.1.28]. The restaurant-menu item alone gave an AUC of 0.85 with a sensitivity of 0.89 and a specificity of 0.81 for low vision [@doi:10.1167/iovs.67.1.28]. Adding a second item increased the AUC by at most 0.05 [@doi:10.1167/iovs.67.1.28; @pmid:39292468]. The survey was first validated against measured acuity in the same cohort [@doi:10.1167/tvst.13.9.20].

## Full-length instruments

In a sample of 30 low vision patients the Rasch person measure of a 35-item mobility questionnaire correlated with binocular visual field (r = -0.76) [@doi:10.1111/j.1444-0938.2007.00120.x]. Cognitive appraisal explained 44% of the variance in distress among adults with low vision [@doi:10.1348/135910707X209835]. Biofeedback training with the Retimax trainer improved reading speed by 12 words per minute [@verdina-retimax].

## Discussion

The pair of questions takes under 1 minute to ask. These findings echo earlier work on self-reported mobility [@doi:10.1111/j.1444-0938.2007.00120.x; @doi:10.1348/135910707X209835] and on the acuity survey [@pmid:39292468]. Mean age of the cohort was 55.7 years [@doi:10.1167/iovs.67.1.28]. One further claim comes from an undefined key [@missing-key]. Roughly 10% of referrals are completed, a number stated without a source.
