## Eligibility criteria (P5, D6)

We included studies of adults (18 years or older) that evaluated a self-report questionnaire or screening item against a measure of visual function or of need for low vision rehabilitation (LVR), and reported diagnostic accuracy (sensitivity, specificity, area under the receiver operating characteristic curve), validation data (reliability, Rasch or other psychometric analysis) or clinical use. Index tests were brief self-report screening items (for example, {the two-item restaurant-menu and keyboard questions}) and full-length instruments ({NEI VFQ-25, VA LV VFQ-48, LVQOL, IVI, GQL-15, Activity Inventory, ...}). Reference standards were measured visual acuity, visual field, a full-length questionnaire, or clinician-judged LVR need. Glaucoma-specific patient-reported outcome studies and guidelines or consensus statements on LVR referral were also eligible{ for the narrative sections only}. We excluded studies of children only, rehabilitation interventions without questionnaire validation or screening data, studies with no questionnaire or screening item, conference abstracts without usable data, case reports and opinion pieces, and reports not in English or French. {Report characteristics: publication year 2000 onward; any publication status.}

For synthesis, studies were grouped by index test (brief screening items versus full-length instruments), by reference standard (visual acuity, visual field, LVR need) and by positivity threshold (P5, D14).

## Certainty of evidence (P15, D24)

We rated the certainty of evidence for sensitivity and specificity of each index test against each reference standard with the GRADE approach for test accuracy (Schünemann 2008; Schünemann 2020), starting at high for cross-sectional or cohort accuracy studies and downgrading for risk of bias, indirectness, inconsistency, imprecision and publication bias. Consequences are expressed as true and false positives and negatives per 1000 people tested at pre-test probabilities of {10%, 30% and 50%}, chosen to represent {a general ophthalmology clinic, a glaucoma clinic and an LVR referral population}. The ratings are summarised in a Summary of Findings table ({Table N}).
