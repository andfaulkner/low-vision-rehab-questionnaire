## AI-use policies

- ICMJE. Recommendations for the Conduct, Reporting, Editing, and Publication of Scholarly Work in Medical Journals. Updated January 2024 (AI section first added May 2023). Section II.A.4 "Artificial Intelligence (AI)-Assisted Technology": disclose use in the cover letter and the manuscript; AI cannot be an author; authors are responsible for AI-generated material and for its accuracy and absence of plagiarism.
- COPE. Authorship and AI tools: COPE position statement. 13 February 2023. AI tools cannot meet authorship requirements; authors must be transparent in the Materials and Methods (or similar) about how the tool was used.
- WAME. Chatbots, Generative AI, and Scholarly Manuscripts: WAME Recommendations. Revised 31 May 2023.
- Glaucoma Today author guidelines (Bryn Mawr Communications): check the current instructions at glaucomatoday.com before submission; they are not ICMJE-based and may not mention AI. Disclose regardless.
