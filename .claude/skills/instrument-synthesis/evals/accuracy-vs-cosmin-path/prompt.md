Two of our included papers: Wu 2026 reports sensitivity 0.89 and specificity 0.81 for the question "In a fast-food restaurant, can you usually read the posted menus?" against VA of 0.3 logMAR or worse, with the best items picked by AUC in the same 385-person sample. Di Maggio 2020 reports Cronbach's alpha 0.98 for the Italian VA LV VFQ-48 in 131 low-vision patients and correlations of 0.39-0.49 with reading speed. Which of these results go into the COSMIN measurement-property table, which go elsewhere, and how do they end up in one recommendation for our glaucoma clinic?
