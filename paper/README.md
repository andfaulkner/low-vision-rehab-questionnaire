## What is provisional, and why

- **Inclusion.** The reviewer has decided 40 of 135 records at title/abstract (columns H and I) and none at full text (J and K). The chain could not wait, so it built a provisional included set (31 reports) from the reviewer's decisions where they exist and from AI suggestions elsewhere (plan rule 6; `analysis/screening/provisional_included.csv`, `basis` column). The DRAFT note at the top of the manuscript, the Figure 1 legend and the PRISMA counts all say so.
- **Every extracted number** is an AI extraction checked by a second AI pass and, in step 10, by verifier subagents against the article text, with three table pages read on the PDFs. It is advisory until the reviewer has read the cited page. `paper/factcheck_report.md` lists every item with its verdict.
- **Catalogue facts** marked "(unverified)" in Tables 1 and 2 (minutes to administer for NEI VFQ-25, Activity Inventory, IVI, GQL-15 and GAL-9; the Activity Inventory task count) were not confirmed against a development paper in the corpus.
- **`[VERIFY]` tags in the manuscript** (4, plus `[UPDATE]` and `[untested]`): the Ernst 2025 referral proportion (ID 130 has no text); the AAO PPP referral criteria (ID 135 has no text); the Box field threshold and PPP alignment; the reviewer-override count and the "counts of corrected values" sentence in the AI disclosure; the Box timing.
- **Search counts.** PubMed 78 and Scopus 88 come from the 4 October 2026 search log; the Scopus translation of the revised string is untested (no API).
- **One source conflict.** Labreche 2018 Table 1 prints the specificity of "difficulty noticing objects off to the side" against a field defect as "65/79 86% (83-72)"; the fraction (0.82) and the percentage (0.86) disagree. The manuscript's Table 3 uses the fraction with a footnote, and step 10 carried the same value into `analysis/extraction/dta.csv` (recorded in `resolutions.csv` as advisory), the synthesis accuracy table and Figure 2, so the package is consistent; the reviewer still confirms the cell.
- **Index question wording.** Wu 2026 prints Q97 as "In a fast-food restaurant, can you usually read the posted menus?" in its tables and Discussion; the abstract omits "usually". The manuscript quotes the table wording.

## Re-running the chain

After step 1 is done and the Screening CSV has been regenerated with the screening-workbook skill:

```
./lvr chain docs/prompts/glaucoma-today-review-2026-10-26-prompt.md --start-from 5
```

This runs steps 5 to 10 (extraction, risk of bias, synthesis, PRISMA and Methods, manuscript, fact-check). Each step appends a status section to `docs/plans/glaucoma-today-review-plan.md` and ends with a `result:` line.

To rebuild only the manuscript products by hand (`S=.claude/skills/manuscript-references/scripts`):

```
python3 -I $S/cite.py check  paper/manuscript.md --bib paper/bibliography.json --manual paper/bibliography_manual.csv --style paper/style_glaucoma_today.json
python3 -I $S/cite.py render paper/manuscript.md --bib paper/bibliography.json --manual paper/bibliography_manual.csv --style paper/style_glaucoma_today.json --numbering paper/reference_numbers.csv
python3 -I $S/wordcount.py   paper/manuscript.md
python3 -I $S/factcheck.py   paper/manuscript.md --bib paper/bibliography.json --manual paper/bibliography_manual.csv \
    --screening data/LVR_screening_sheet_2_Screening.csv --text-dir data/articles_text --extraction analysis/extraction \
    --prisma paper/prisma_counts.md --map doi:10.1167/tvst.13.9.20=analysis/extraction/sources/Wu_2024_TVST_PMC11412607.txt \
    --out paper/factcheck_report.md --json paper/factcheck_report.json
python3 -I .claude/skills/prisma-reporting/scripts/checklist_status.py paper/checklists/*.md --strict
```
