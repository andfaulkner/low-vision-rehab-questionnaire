Files: `quadas2.csv`, `quadas2_table.md`, `quadas2_traffic_light.png`, `quadas2_summary.png`, `cosmin.csv`, `cosmin_ratings.csv`, `cosmin_table.md`, `agree2_narrative.md`.

## Dominant problems

All five accuracy studies are High risk of bias overall, and none is Low on more than two domains. The recurring problems are (1) data-driven selection of items or cut-offs in the same sample that evaluates them (D2: Wu 2026, Wu 2024, Pérez-Mañá 2022); (2) spectrum-enriched or two-gate samples (D1: Pérez-Mañá 2022 contrasts impaired patients with younger healthy controls, the Wu cohort was recruited to cover the full acuity range with 68.6% worse than 0.3 logMAR, Labreche 2018 was deliberately enriched, and Gresset 2002 verified all positives plus a sample of negatives, so its accuracy cannot be applied to an unselected population); (3) reference standards that are acuity-only (Wu, Gresset, Pérez-Mañá) or retrospective chart composites (Labreche 2018, D3 High), which cannot see field or contrast loss, the typical glaucoma deficit; (4) unreported or long intervals and unblinded reference standards (D4 and D3 Unclear for most); and (5) index items embedded in a longer survey or administered by a different mode from the intended use (Wu: items answered inside the 100-item survey, interviewer-led; Pérez-Mañá: telephone vs face-to-face). All 39 COSMIN property ratings are Doubtful except one Inadequate (Sumi 2003 structural validity, 102 patients for 35 items); the common causes are no a-priori hypotheses, unreported stability or administration conditions in test-retest, and Rasch analyses on small or unreported samples. Responsiveness studies are all uncontrolled pre-post designs.

## Per-study judgements step 9 must mention

- **ID 92 Wu 2026 (n = 385), overall High.** D2 High: items, pairs and triplets chosen by AUC ranking over 100 items in the same 385 people, no validation sample, and answered within the 100-item survey. D1, D3, D4 Unclear (recruitment method, blinding, order and interval not reported in the paper; protocol deferred to the earlier survey paper). Applicability concern for patients (68.6% VA worse than 0.3 logMAR) and for the index test. A3 Low: VA at 0.3 and 1.0 logMAR is an accepted proxy, but the reference used habitual-correction, often binocular, VA. So the two headline items (Q97 menu, Q30 keyboard) are derivation-sample estimates and should be called optimistic.
- **ID 128 Wu 2024 (same 385 adults as ID 92), overall High, poor fit.** An IRT estimation study with no sensitivity or specificity; QUADAS-2 fits poorly (D2 q2 and D3 q3 NA). All four domains High, mainly because of convenience sampling, same-sample model fitting and mixed VA sources (some Snellen, some from records). The subagent suggested COSMIN may suit it better. Count ID 92 and 128 as one dataset; the plot and weighted summary count them twice, so the summary percentages overstate agreement across independent evidence.
- **ID 119 Pérez-Mañá 2022 (n = 365), overall High.** D1 High (two-gate impaired vs healthy, controls younger), D2 High (cut-off 105 or 106 chosen by ROC in the same sample, sensitivity 95.4%, specificity 91.8%, mode differed by site). The index test is a 25-item PROM, not a brief screen, and it separates VA-defined groups rather than flagging LVR need.
- **ID 27 Labreche 2018 (n = 89), overall High.** D3 High (retrospective chart review, composite reference with contradictory VA threshold wording, p2 vs Table 1 footnote); abstract n (86) disagrees with Results (89). Applicability High (over 65, enriched clinics, falls-risk target).
- **ID 79 Gresset 2002 (n = 65 of 144 eligible), overall High.** D1 and D4 High: verification depended on the screening answers, 45.1% participation. D2 and D3 Low (ETDRS, examiner blind to answers). The two HALS questions are the only brief self-report items with an acceptable reference, but the authors state the estimates cannot be applied to an unselected population.
- **Guidelines.** ID 38 (ESO): moderate confidence as a secondary source; the questionnaire evidence is one Medium-RoB study. ID 41 (Colombian CPG): low confidence for accuracy claims; NEI VFQ-25 recommended on psychometric evidence labelled "high" while most evidence was low or very low. ID 93 (Chou 2009) not appraised.
- **ID 85 Smith 2013 (LVIM).** Qualitative content-validity study with therapists only; the COSMIN template has no content-validity box, so no rating. Authors' own limits: unpiloted questionnaire, cursory analysis of focus group 1.
- **Questionnaire validation (COSMIN).** For the full-length instruments the review contrasts with the two-item screen (VA LV VFQ-48: 66, 74, 105; NEI VFQ-25: 65; IVI: 72; LVQOL/SLVQOL: 26, 119; APEDS-VFQ: 5; Activity Inventory: 46, 86, 88; VA-13: 39; EQ-5D vision bolt-on: 114), no property is better than Doubtful, so "validated" must be worded as "evidence of limited methodological quality", not as established.
