# AGREE II narrative appraisals (advisory; AI-drafted, reviewer to confirm)

Six-domain narrative only, no numeric scores. Drafted by sonnet subagents from the article text; page numbers are the `[page N]` markers in `data/articles_text/`.

## ID 38: ESO guideline on visual impairment in stroke (Rowe 2025)

- **Scope and purpose:** objective, PICO questions and target users stated (adult stroke survivors; providers, survivors, carers) [page 1].
- **Stakeholder involvement:** multidisciplinary group (stroke medicine, neurology, neuropsychology, ophthalmology, orthoptics) [page 4]; patient or carer members not reported; no user piloting.
- **Rigour of development:** the strongest domain. Databases to March 2023, dual screening, QUADAS-2 and GRADE, Delphi voting, external review [pages 5-6]. No update procedure reported.
- **Clarity of presentation:** specific graded recommendations in a summary table; expert-consensus statements kept separate from evidence-based ones [page 6].
- **Applicability:** feasibility of screening discussed; tools "available free of charge" [page 8]; barriers, resource and audit criteria largely not reported.
- **Editorial independence:** funded by the ESO [page 68]; competing interests deferred to Supplemental Table 1, which is not in the text.
- **Questionnaire evidence:** the Cerebral Vision Screening Questionnaire rests on one study rated QUADAS-2 "Medium"; sensitivity and specificity are for single symptom items (e.g. reading problems 83.9% and 79.1% [page 15]). The guideline endorses no specific questionnaire.
- **Overall (advisory):** moderate confidence as a secondary source; go to the primary study for accuracy figures.

## ID 41: Colombian low vision CPG (Oviedo-Cáceres 2023)

- **Scope and purpose:** objective stated (diagnosis, functional rehabilitation, follow-up) [page 3]; covers ages 7-18 and over 18; screening-questionnaire accuracy is not a question it asks.
- **Stakeholder involvement:** multidisciplinary group, 22 people with low vision and relatives, two external expert consultations [page 3]; influence on recommendations not reported.
- **Rigour of development:** five databases plus grey sources, QUADAS/AMSTAR/Cochrane tools, GRADE and Evidence-to-Decision [pages 3-4]. "Most quality of evidence ... was low or very low" [page 4]. Search strings, flow diagram, per-study risk of bias and update procedure not reported.
- **Clarity of presentation:** recommendations in Table 1 with certainty and strength [page 5]; no separate key-recommendations box.
- **Applicability:** barriers and costs discussed per recommendation (NEI-VFQ "can take additional time in the office" [page 10]); no audit criteria or toolkit.
- **Editorial independence:** co-funded by a university and two town halls [pages 10-11]; "Conflict of interests: None" [page 11]; no statement that funders did not influence content.
- **NEI VFQ-25 recommendation:** strong, certainty labelled "high" [page 10], but the evidence is psychometric, not diagnostic accuracy, and the "high" label sits oddly with the guideline's own statement that most evidence was low or very low.
- **Overall (advisory):** low confidence as evidence on questionnaire accuracy; at most a background statement on VFQ-25 use.
